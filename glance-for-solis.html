Glance for Solis

See your solar system at a glance. Glance for Solis puts live PV generation and battery state-of-charge on your Home Screen and Lock Screen, so you always know what your panels are producing and how full your battery is - without opening an app.

Live PV power and battery SoC. Right on your Home Screen.

1 Sign in with your SolisCloud credentials
2 Add a Home Screen or Lock Screen widget
3 See live generation and battery at a glance

Unofficial companion for SolisCloud users. Not affiliated with Ginlong or Solis.

Live Widgets, Front and Center

  • Home Screen widgets in small, medium, and large sizes
  • Lock Screen widgets (circular and rectangular) for instant battery and power readouts
  • Compatible with iPhone, iPad, and Mac

Real-Time PV Power

See what your panels are producing right now. Live PV output in kilowatts, refreshed directly from your SolisCloud account, so a glance at your Home Screen tells you whether the sun is working for you.

Battery State of Charge

Know exactly how full your battery is, with color-coded indicators that make status readable at a glance. Green when you're full, warmer tones as it drains - no mental arithmetic required.

Live Auto-Refresh

A one-time in-app purchase unlocks live auto-refresh across every widget size. Without it, widgets still install and display your system, but data stays frozen - so you see exactly what you'll get before you decide.

Last-Update Timestamp

Every widget shows when its data was last fetched, so you always know whether you're looking at fresh numbers or the last reading your device could pull.

Private and Direct

  • Your SolisCloud credentials stay on-device
  • Glance talks to SolisCloud directly from your iPhone, iPad, or Mac
  • No middle server, no account signup, no tracking, no analytics, no ads

Unofficial Companion

Not affiliated with Ginlong or Solis. Glance for Solis is an unofficial third-party companion app for SolisCloud users. You'll need a SolisCloud account and API access to use it.

Requirements

  • iPhone, iPad, or Mac
  • A SolisCloud account with API access
  • One-time in-app purchase for live auto-refresh (optional)
