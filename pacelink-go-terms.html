1. What PaceLink Go Does

The App helps people travelling together see their relative positions and estimated distance and time gaps. It works alongside your chosen navigation app. It is not a turn-by-turn navigation service, emergency service, safety system, or a guarantee of another person's location or wellbeing.

2. Safe and Responsible Use

Set up trips and invitations before driving. Operate the App only when it is safe and lawful to do so; stop safely or ask a passenger to help. Never speed, change lanes, or alter your driving to match a displayed gap. Follow road signs, traffic laws, and the conditions around you.

Use the App only with people who choose to participate. Do not use it for covert tracking, stalking, harassment, impersonation, or any unlawful purpose. Do not bypass access controls, misuse invitation credentials, interfere with the service, or attempt to reuse a consumed purchase.

3. Trips and Invitations

No account is required. Choose a display name and create or join a trip using an invitation link or code. Trips support up to five participants. Anyone with a working invitation can attempt to join, so keep invitations within your group.

Joining a trip shares your display name and live trip information with its participants. You may leave at any time, and the host may end the trip for everyone. Trips also expire automatically. An internet connection is needed for the server to receive a leave or end request. Retention and deletion timing are explained in the Privacy Policy.

4. Welcome Trips and Optional Purchases

Joining is free. The current version offers hosts two welcome trips of up to 12 hours each. After the allowance is used, a consumable 24-hour Trip Pass is required to host a new trip. A host can also purchase a pass to add 24 hours to a trip that is still active. The trip's running period begins when its first guest joins; an unused invitation can expire.

Trip Passes are one-time purchases, not auto-renewing subscriptions. Each verified purchase can be consumed once. Ending a trip early does not turn a consumed pass into a reusable credit. The current price, currency, and purchase details are displayed by the App Store before you confirm.

Apple handles payment. Refund requests are handled through Apple's purchase support process, subject to applicable law. If a purchase succeeds but the App cannot apply it, contact us for help. Nothing in these terms limits your statutory refund or consumer rights.

5. Requirements and Availability

The App requires a compatible iPhone running iOS 26 or later, location permission, and internet access. Background updates require appropriate location permission. Live Activities, Dynamic Island, and CarPlay availability depend on your device, vehicle, system settings, and Apple's platform support.

GPS errors, poor reception, battery-saving settings, system limits, outages, or other participants' settings may delay, interrupt, or make information inaccurate. Distance and time gaps are estimates. We cannot promise continuous availability or error-free results. Your carrier's data charges may apply.

6. Local History and Privacy

Past-trip summaries are saved locally and do not contain a GPS route. Deleting the App may remove your local summaries and preferences. We do not provide a server history archive or recovery service for these summaries. The Privacy Policy explains the information processed during trips, retained purchase records, usage counts, and your rights.
