The Box - Gravity Maze - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application The Box - Gravity Maze ("the App"), developed by Daniel Szlaski ("we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, and shares information when you use the App. The Box - Gravity Maze is distributed exclusively through the Apple App Store for iOS devices.

1. Information We Collect

The Box - Gravity Maze does not collect personal data. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information.

The App does not use third-party advertising services or analytics SDKs. No data is transmitted to third-party servers by the App. The App contains no ads of any kind.

2. In-App Purchases

The Box - Gravity Maze offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to unlock the full game beyond level 15 and unlimited weekly challenge attempts. All purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information such as credit card numbers or billing addresses.

When you make a purchase, we receive a purchase receipt from Apple to verify your transaction and unlock the corresponding features. This receipt data contains transaction identifiers and entitlement information but does not include personal financial details. Apple's handling of payment data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Game Center

The App integrates with Apple Game Center for weekly challenge leaderboards. Your Game Center display name and scores are managed by Apple. We do not collect or store any Game Center data on our servers. Apple's handling of Game Center data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your personal data to any third parties.

5. Data Retention

We do not store any user data on our servers. All game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

6. Children's Privacy

The Box - Gravity Maze is rated 4+ on the App Store and is suitable for all ages. The App does not contain any advertising, does not collect personal information, and does not include any content unsuitable for children. All in-app purchases are handled through Apple's parental controls and require authentication as configured on the device. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable age-related regulations.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data. Since we do not collect personal data, there is no user data held by us to access, modify, or delete.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
