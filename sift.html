iOS & iPadOS Game

Sift

Tilt. Sift. Save every gem. A physics puzzle where gravity is your only tool - sort diamonds from junk before they spill.

A glass vessel full of precious diamonds - buried under junk. Bolts, paperclips, teddy bears, donuts… the clutter never ends. Tilt your phone, use real-world gravity to sift out the trash - and keep every sparkling gem safely inside.

Easy to Learn, Impossible to Master

Pure tilt controls - no buttons, no tapping. Just you, gravity, and the laws of physics. Pick up your device and start playing in seconds.

Escalating Challenge

New junk types unlock as you progress: heavy rocks, slippery paperclips, stubborn bolts, and floating balloons. Sticky poop glues objects together - shake to break free. Fragile bombs detonate after three hard strikes.

Five Unique Vessels

Classic bottle, Erlenmeyer flask, round-bottom flask, tall flask, and wide-open beaker - each with its own physics personality. The vessel changes every five stages, keeping you on your toes.

Gorgeous Visuals

Faceted gem shaders in cyan, amber, ruby, and emerald. Realistic glass lighting, parallax bokeh background, and delicate crystal clink sound effects bring every session to life.

Scoring & Stats

Preserve every diamond for a Perfect Stage bonus. Track lifetime stats: diamonds saved, junk sorted, and more. How far can you go?
