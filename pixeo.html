Pixeo

Pixels in. Guesses out. Nostalgia all around. A retro pixel art guessing game where blurry blobs sharpen into crisp NES-style reveals - guess fast, score big.

Download on the App Store

Can you name it before the pixels do? Each round, a fuzzy pixelated image slowly sharpens - from a mysterious mosaic of chunky squares to a crisp, NES-style pixel art reveal. Guess early, score big. Wait too long and your points melt away.

Easy to Play, Hard to Master

Every round starts with a blurry, low-resolution image. Pick the right answer from four choices and rack up points - but the longer you wait, the lower your score drops. Three wrong guesses and it's game over.

HYPER-FOCUS Mode

Nail three correct guesses while the image is still blurry and unlock a 2x score multiplier that lights up the screen and kicks the music into overdrive. Stay sharp and ride the streak.

Day & Night Modes

PIXEO knows what time it is. Play during the day for a bright, upbeat LCD world with bouncy chiptune beats. Fire it up at night for dark skies, mysterious vibes, and a whole different set of animals.

Retro to the Core

Designed to look and feel like a classic 1980s handheld LCD console. Real pixel fonts, NES-style color palettes, scanline overlays - maximum nostalgia with modern puzzle game polish.

Original Chiptune Soundtrack

Every sound is synthesized in real-time - no audio files. Day theme: bright, 112 BPM, pure NES energy. Night theme: slower, moodier, with cricket chirps layered in. Music speeds up as rounds get harder.

Compete Globally

Your high score gets submitted to Game Center leaderboards. Think you've got top-tier pixel recognition skills? Prove it. Start with 29 hand-crafted pixel art animals across day and night modes.

Sharpen Your Eyes

Free to play. Available now on iPhone and iPad.
