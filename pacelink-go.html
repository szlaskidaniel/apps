Keep Your Navigation

Keep using Apple Maps, Google Maps, Waze, or your preferred navigation app. PaceLink Go runs alongside it to show the group's relative positions, distance gaps, and estimated time gaps.

Live Activity & CarPlay

Check your group from the Lock Screen, Dynamic Island on supported iPhones, and Live Activities on compatible CarPlay systems. Updates depend on your connection and system settings.

A Trip, Not an Account

Pick a display name and share an invitation link or short code. Up to five people can travel together. Your name and live trip information are shared with the people who join, so send invitations only to people you trust.

Location for the Journey

Share your location while travelling together. Leave the trip whenever you want to stop sharing, or end it for everyone as the host.

Past Trips on Your iPhone

Look back at distance travelled, time moving and stopped, speed statistics, and gaps to your companions. Summaries include companion names and stay in local app storage. No saved route or GPS history is included.

Simple Trip Passes

Joining is free. Try hosting with two welcome trips of up to 12 hours each per eligible device. After that, a one-time Trip Pass gives you 24 hours. Add another day when you need it. No subscription. See offer details.

Ready when you are.

Requires an iPhone with iOS 26 or later, location permission, and an internet connection. Allow background location to keep sharing while using navigation. Set up your trip before driving and check the app only when safe.
