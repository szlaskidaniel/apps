YOUR HOME. YOUR DATA.

It stays personal.
By design.

LowBat runs battery processing on your device. Local notifications and Live Activities are created and updated on-device, too. We don’t operate a LowBat backend or a remote notification server.

Read the privacy policy ↗
  • Direct communication with your Hue Bridge over local Wi-Fi.
  • HomeKit readings handled through Apple’s HomeKit framework.
  • No LowBat account, tracking, analytics or advertising.
  • Battery data is not uploaded to LowBat servers.

A small setup detail: if local Hue discovery fails, LowBat may contact Philips Hue’s discovery service to find your bridge. Purchases use Apple’s App Store. Neither is a LowBat battery-processing server.
