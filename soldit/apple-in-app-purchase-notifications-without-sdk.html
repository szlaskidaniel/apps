How to receive Apple In-App Purchase notifications without an SDK

You can add developer alerts to an app that already sells through StoreKit without submitting another build. When a purchase or subscription changes, Apple sends a signed event from its servers to the HTTPS address you register. SoldIt! receives that event and alerts your iPhone.

What you need from your existing app

Have the live app’s bundle ID and access to its App Information page in App Store Connect. Your app continues to handle checkout and grant access to purchases. The notification setup connects a second destination for information about what happened after checkout.

Set up the receiving end

Add the bundle ID in SoldIt! and copy the webhook URL from that app’s settings. For an app with an unused production notification URL, register the address in App Store Connect and select Version 2. If your subscription backend already receives Apple’s events, changing that URL redirects its traffic. Confirm a supported delivery path before editing it.

Recognize the events in your feed

  • One-time purchase. A customer buys a consumable or a permanent unlock. Apple can send a ONE_TIME_CHARGE event.
  • Subscription renewal. A DID_RENEW event tells you an existing subscription renewed, even when the customer hasn’t opened your app.
  • Refund. A REFUND event means Apple granted a refund. A refund request alone leaves the sales total unchanged.
