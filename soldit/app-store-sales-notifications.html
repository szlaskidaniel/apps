How to get instant notifications for App Store sales

A customer buys a lifetime upgrade in your app. To see that sale as an iPhone banner, you need two things in place. Apple needs a destination for the purchase event, and SoldIt! needs permission to show the alert on your phone.

Connect your app to SoldIt!

  1. Sign in with Apple in SoldIt! and tap Add Your First App. Enter the bundle ID of your live App Store app, confirm the app name, and tap Create.
  2. In that app’s settings in SoldIt!, tap Copy URL under Webhook URL. Each app has its own address.
  3. In App Store Connect, select the same app and open App Information. If the production server URL is empty, enter the SoldIt! URL, choose Version 2, and save. If a service already uses that field, keep its URL in place until you have confirmed how both services will receive events.

Choose a banner for each sale

In SoldIt! settings, open Notifications. Switch off Live Activity to reveal Push Notifications, turn it on, and tap Save. Allow notifications when iOS asks. If you previously declined, open the iPhone’s notification settings for SoldIt! and enable banners.

Check the next purchase

After the next paid In-App Purchase or subscription renewal, open the app’s Activity in SoldIt!. If the event appears there but the phone stayed quiet, delivery from Apple worked. Check whether a Focus silenced the banner or SoldIt!’s Quiet Hours grouped it into a later digest.

If the feed is empty too, compare the saved production URL with the URL for that app in SoldIt! and check that the app is active.
