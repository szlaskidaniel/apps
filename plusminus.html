PlusMinus

Turn math into an exciting adventure! Drag, drop and solve number puzzles - the fun way for kids to master addition and subtraction.

A fast-paced number puzzle that feels like a game - because it is one. Kids practice real math skills without even realizing it. The combination of time pressure and strategic tile placement means kids don't just calculate - they plan, prioritize, and adapt.

Drag, Drop and Solve

Numbered tiles appear on the board - some positive, some negative. Drag them into equation slots to match the target number. Two equations run at once, so kids learn to think ahead and juggle multiple problems.

Progressive difficulty

Progressive difficulty that grows with your child. It starts easy, but tiles spawn faster as levels climb. Fill all 5 slots for a bonus score multiplier. 3 lives per round - exciting without being too punishing.

Learning That Feels Like Play

Most math apps feel like homework. PlusMinus feels like a game. Kids practice real addition and subtraction skills without even realizing it. Built for children with bright design and large, easy-to-tap tiles.

Game Center Leaderboards

Track improvement over time and compare scores with friends through Game Center. Works offline too - play anywhere, anytime, no internet required.
